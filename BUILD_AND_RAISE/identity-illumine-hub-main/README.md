# Identity Illumine Hub

Create a professional, modern, and fully responsive website using all the information provided in the attached PDF. Carefully extract and use the exact business name, logo, services, descriptions, contact details, Instagram handle, address, and location from the PDF. Do not invent or change any details.

Design requirements:

Use a premium, clean, and visually elegant layout.

Follow the brand colours and visual identity shown in the PDF.

Use a deep maroon, burgundy, cream, and gold colour palette if consistent with the supplied branding.

Maintain good spacing, typography, contrast, and visual hierarchy.

Ensure the website works perfectly on desktops, tablets, and mobile phones.

Add smooth scrolling, subtle animations, hover effects, and a sticky navigation bar.

Keep the design sophisticated and uncluttered.

Website sections:

Header and navigation
Include the business logo and navigation links for Home, About, Services, Portfolio, and Contact. Add a mobile hamburger menu.

Hero section
Create an attractive first screen featuring the business name, a short introduction based on the PDF, and prominent call-to-action buttons such as “Explore Our Services,” “View Instagram,” and “Contact Us.”

About section
Present a concise and professional introduction to the business using the information in the PDF.

Services section
Clearly display every service mentioned in the PDF. Use stylish service cards with suitable icons or high-quality images. Include each service’s exact name and relevant description. Make the section easy to browse on mobile devices.

Instagram portfolio section
Create a dedicated visual portfolio area with an elegant Instagram-style gallery grid. Add a clear “View More on Instagram” button linked to the exact Instagram account provided in the PDF. If live Instagram embedding is unavailable, use attractive image placeholders that can easily be replaced later. Do not invent Instagram posts or customer work.

Contact section
Display the exact phone number, WhatsApp number, email address, Instagram handle, business address, and working hours available in the PDF. Make phone numbers, email addresses, WhatsApp, and Instagram links clickable.

Interactive location map
Embed a responsive Google Map using the exact business location from the PDF. Include a “Get Directions” button that opens the location in Google Maps.

Footer
Include the logo, business name, quick navigation links, contact information, social-media link, and copyright text.

Additional requirements:

Add floating WhatsApp and “Back to Top” buttons.

Use semantic HTML and accessible labels.

Optimize images and loading performance.

Include appropriate page title, description, favicon, and SEO metadata.

Ensure there is no horizontal scrolling or overlapping content on mobile.

Use real details from the PDF throughout the website.

Do not add fake reviews, prices, achievements, statistics, addresses, or contact information.

Produce a complete, polished website—not merely a design mock-up.
